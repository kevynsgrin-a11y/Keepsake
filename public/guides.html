  • How to record family stories — The method: audio, video, and the habit of dating everything.
  • Family reunion questions — The one room where every version of the family story is present.
  • Holiday gathering questions — Thanksgiving and Christmas — the few days a year every generation is at one table.
  • Family recipe questions — The questions that save the dish the card gets wrong.
  • Family milestones, by age — What to capture at every age, from first steps to the last reunions.
  • Memory box ideas: what to keep — What to keep, what it replaces, and how to label it so it survives you.
  • Baby's first year: the keepsake checklist — The first year is the one families most wish they had captured better.
  • Preserving old family photos — How to keep what survives — and what to do about the box nobody can identify.
